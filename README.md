Featured projects
Medicare Geographic Variation

County-level analysis of CMS Medicare spending and utilization, framed around health equity. Built with SQL, pandas, and Power BI to show where geographic differences in care point to access gaps rather than need. Tools: SQL, Python (pandas), Power BI

Indiana Social Determinants of Health Regression

Modeled health outcomes across all 92 Indiana counties by merging Census ACS poverty data with CDC PLACES outcomes on FIPS code. Food insecurity emerged as the dominant predictor. Explored why diabetes prevalence in low-access counties partly reflects who gets tested rather than who is affected, and mapped the results with a geopandas choropleth. Tools: Python (pandas, scikit-learn, geopandas), 5-fold cross-validation

Supply Chain Delivery Performance Analysis

Diagnosed late-delivery patterns in the DataCo Global supply chain dataset. Established that late delivery is systemic rather than geographic and is driven by shipping mode, pointing to a gap between promised and actual delivery dates. Built interactive Power BI reporting for non-technical stakeholders. Tools: SQL (DuckDB / JupySQL), Python, Power BI
